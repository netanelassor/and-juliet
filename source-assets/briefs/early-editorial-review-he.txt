## 5. הבריף המדויק שהייתי נותן לכלי שבנה את הספר

אפשר להעתיק את זה כפי שהוא:

# & Juliet — Children's Picture Book Editorial Redesign

## Objective

Redesign the existing Hebrew book **"ויוליה? הסיפור עוד לא נגמר!"** into a professionally illustrated children's picture book for a 6-year-old.

The current result has beautiful illustrations, consistent characters, and a strong visual identity. However, it feels like a digital interactive story viewer rather than a traditionally published children's book.

**The goal is NOT to redesign the illustrations or rewrite the entire story. The goal is to improve storytelling flow, pacing, editorial layout, and the overall picture-book experience.**

## 1. Story Structure

- Keep the original Hebrew storyline, humor, character personalities, and natural dialogues.
- Preserve the important plot events and their chronological order in the Broadway production.
- Reduce the current 48 individual story pages to approximately 36 pages organized into 18 narrative spreads.
- Combine neighboring pages that describe the same scene or conversation.
- Remove redundant dialogue, repeated explanations, and unnecessary transitions.
- Do not summarize the story into disconnected short sentences. Maintain a natural, continuous narrative.
- Aim for approximately 80–130 Hebrew words per spread, depending on the scene. Dramatic moments can use significantly fewer.
- Maintain natural Hebrew suited to reading aloud to a 6-year-old.
- Preserve emotional development, humorous interactions, and the child-friendly handling of mature themes.
- Keep the alternating storytelling perspective between Juliet's adventure and Shakespeare and Anne's discussions.

Every spread must function as a complete narrative beat while flowing naturally into the next spread.

## 2. Book Structure

Final format: 30 × 30 cm, square, printed, right-to-left Hebrew.

Target approximately 40 interior pages:

- 2 pages: title and personal dedication.
- 36 pages: 18 story spreads.
- 2 pages: ending and music reference.

Remove the duplicate title page and unnecessary introductory or instructional pages.

The reader should enter the story almost immediately after the dedication.

## 3. Editorial Design — Critical

STOP treating every page as a separate UI card.

Remove the repetitive layout of:

- Full-width image at the top.
- Rounded cream text container underneath.
- Large heading on every page.
- Numbered story indicator.
- Play icon or interactive-looking music component.
- UI-like separators and decorative bars.

Instead, create a dynamic, high-quality children's picture book layout.

Use different compositions:

- Full-bleed artwork with text integrated into quiet areas.
- Narrative-heavy pages with smaller illustrations.
- Connected scenes or illustration vignettes.
- Dramatic illustrated double-page spreads with minimal text.

The text and artwork must feel like one composition, not two independent interface components.

Not every page needs a large illustration. An illustration should exist only when it contributes to storytelling, emotion, visual rhythm, or character recognition.

## 4. Illustrations

Preserve the existing illustration style and character designs.

The visual direction is **Broadway Pop × Hip-Hop × Modern Renaissance Storybook**.

Keep:

- Modern streetwear mixed with Renaissance costumes.
- Sneakers, colorful fabrics, stage lighting, neon and theatrical scenery.
- Bold theatrical perspectives and expressive faces.
- The Broadway-inspired set design.
- Strong, consistent visual identities for every character.

Reuse the strongest existing illustrations wherever possible.

Avoid generating replacement illustrations unless combining scenes creates a genuine narrative need.

Avoid cropping important faces, gestures, costumes, or scenery.

Each spread should be deliberately art-directed, not generated from a fixed page template.

## 5. Typography

Use professional Hebrew children's book typography.

- Narrative text: Varela Round or another highly legible Hebrew book font.
- Main title and occasional dramatic typography: Heebo ExtraBold or Rubik Bold.
- Text should be large enough for comfortable reading on a physical 30 × 30 cm page.
- Prefer approximately 21–24 pt for body text, adjusted to the actual composition.
- Use comfortable line spacing and consistent RTL punctuation.
- Avoid unnecessary bold formatting.
- Do not use large headings for every scene.
- Let the storytelling, not the headings, carry the transitions.

Text must never compete visually with important parts of an illustration.

## 6. Music Integration

Keep the exact English song titles from the Broadway cast recording, aligned with the correct scene.

Music references should be discreet editorial footnotes, not interactive UI elements.

For example:

♫ Confident

Place the song name in small typography near the bottom or outside the main story text.

Do not display large play buttons, track-number badges, or audio-player components.

Include one final music index with the complete song list and a Spotify QR code.

## 7. Required Editorial Workflow

Before implementing the redesign:

1. Review the existing PDF page by page.
2. Propose a new 18-spread storyboard mapping existing pages to new spreads.
3. Identify which existing illustrations will be retained, combined, reduced, or removed.
4. Rewrite only where necessary to improve narrative flow and reduce repetition.
5. Create three representative sample spreads with different page layouts.
6. Apply the approved editorial system consistently across the entire book.
7. Export a print-ready PDF with proper RTL handling, safe margins, bleed, and no text or essential artwork crossing the gutter.

## Final Quality Standard

The result should feel like a beautiful children's book sold in a bookstore, with professional storytelling, art direction, visual pacing, and typography.

It should NOT feel like a collection of slides, an interactive story app, a presentation, or a website exported to PDF.

**Preserve the soul of the existing book. Change the editorial structure and composition, not its personality.**

## 6. עוד שינוי אחד שהייתי עושה

הייתי מוציא את עמוד "איך קוראים ומקשיבים?" שמופיע בתחילת הספר. הוא מאוד שימושי, אבל מרגיש כמו הוראות שימוש באפליקציה. בספר ילדים לא צריך להסביר איך קוראים אותו.

במקומו הייתי משאיר את ההקדשה המקסימה לרון, ומיד אחריה מתחיל את הסיפור.

את שמות השירים הייתי שומר בתחתית הכפולות בטיפוגרפיה קטנה ועדינה, ואת קוד ה-QR ואת רשימת השירים המלאה מרכז בסוף הספר.

השורה התחתונה: אני לא הייתי משנה את הסגנון הגרפי בכלל. הייתי משנה את החשיבה מ־48 עמודים שצריך למלא בטקסט ובאיור, ל־18 כפולות סיפור שכל אחת מהן מקבלת בימוי אמנותי משלה.

זה ההבדל שלדעתי יהפוך את התוצאה ממצגת מאוירת יפה לספר ילדים אמיתי, כזה שכיף להחזיק, לדפדף בו ולהקריא לרון שוב ושוב.
